Marguerite came to C from the formal-methods side: separation logic,
Hoare-style proof, and a PhD spent discharging verification conditions one
`malloc` at a time. What she brings to this course is a settled opinion that
none of the tools we study are "the answer" — each is a different bet about
which C programs are worth the annotation cost, and the interesting question
is always what a given tool refuses to let you say.

She teaches the theory weeks (2, 3, 6, 7) and sets the comparative report.
